DESCRIPTION  (plain text, paste into Zenodo's "Description" box)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
We prove three theorems about the standard Li-type observation of
finite symmetric zero multisets, and record two main corollaries.

Let H_sym be the class of finite multisets in the open critical strip
symmetric under rho -> rho-bar and rho -> 1-rho, and let P(Z) = 1
iff every atom has Re(rho) = 1/2.

Theorem A (exact Li collision): for every m >= 1 there exist Z+, Z-
in H_sym with P(Z+) = 1, P(Z-) = 0, and identical standard Li
observations Li_j(Z+) = Li_j(Z-) for j = 1, ..., m, realised by
explicit rational-arithmetic witnesses.

Theorem B (no uniform separation): no fixed finite initial segment of
standard Li values enforces a strictly positive gap between P = 1 and
P = 0 within H_sym.

Theorem C (incomparability): the standard Li map O_fin^(K) and the
sampled counting map O_theta are incomparable in the information
refinement preorder on H_sym.

Corollary D (Andersson): agreement with any fixed finite initial
collection of Euler factors of the Riemann zeta function does not
force a Helson zeta function's zeros to lie on the critical line
(existential obstruction pair, using Andersson 2024, Theorem 5 and
Corollary 3).

Corollary E (joint obstruction): the combined observation
O_fin^(K) x O_theta also admits an exact information obstruction.

None of these results assume or imply the Riemann Hypothesis. The
class H_sym consists of finite formal multisets; the actual zero set
of zeta is infinite and subject to additional analytic structure not
imposed on H_sym.
