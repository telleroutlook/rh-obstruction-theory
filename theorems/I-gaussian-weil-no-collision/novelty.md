# Novelty — Theorem I

The new point is not the Lindemann-Weierstrass theorem itself but its exact
role in the finite observation model: for real algebraic Gaussian/Hermite-Gaussian
tests, the off-line quartet and on-line pairs produce exponentials with distinct
algebraic exponents, so the integer balancing mechanism available for rational
Li observations is impossible.

This gives a proved negative answer, for this test class, to the natural portability
question raised by Paper A Open Problem 2. It also sharply separates:

- rational Li observations, where exact integer collisions exist; from
- algebraic Gaussian Weil observations, where no nondegenerate exact integer
  collision exists.

The theorem does not claim novelty for Lindemann-Weierstrass and does not solve
the compactly supported Paley-Wiener case.
