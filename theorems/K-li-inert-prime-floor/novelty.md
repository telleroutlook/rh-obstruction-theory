# Novelty — Theorem K

The theorem isolates the inert-prime mechanism behind the resource-bounded Li
collision: an inert denominator prime cannot occur in any rational on-line
conductor `4a^2+b^2`, so a pole in the off-line Chebyshev data forces the
off-line integer coefficient to be divisible by a growing power of `p`.

This gives an explicit infinite family for which the lower bound is uniform
over every rational on-line node set. It does not solve the split-only cases
that constitute the remaining core of Open Problem 1.
