# Limitations — Theorem K

1. The theorem covers only off-line parameters `u_0` with an inert prime
   `p≡3 mod 4` in the denominator.

2. The lower bound has a delay `N` depending on the residue of `ξ`; it is
   exponential in `m` only after that fixed delay.

3. The bound is uniform over rational on-line nodes but not over all off-line
   orbits. Split-only denominators remain outside the theorem.

4. Only the off-line coefficient `q` is bounded. A separate argument is needed
   to bound all on-line coefficients or the total atom count.

5. This is finite exact arithmetic. It uses no zero of `ζ`, no analytic
   zero-counting law, and no RH-equivalent hypothesis.
