# Novelty — Theorem J

The mathematical content is standard integer linear algebra, but its role is
new in the finite Li-collision problem: it identifies the least possible
off-line multiplicity as an exact lattice index (equivalently, a ratio of
determinantal divisors). This converts the resource-bounded Open Problem 1 into
a quantitative arithmetic question about Smith invariants of Chebyshev-value
matrices.

The theorem does not solve that arithmetic question and does not claim a
uniform exponential lower bound.
