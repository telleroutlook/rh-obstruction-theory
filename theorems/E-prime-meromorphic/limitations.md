# Limitations — Theorem E' (E-prime-meromorphic)

1. **E'-neg is PROOF-DRAFT**: the meromorphic IFT Jacobian step (Step 1, proof.md §2)
   has the right structure but has not been written out explicitly with residue
   coordinates. Until this step is complete, E'-neg is not INDEPENDENTLY-CHECKED.

2. **E'-pos is OPEN**: the Marty + Vitali + argument-principle proof has not been
   executed. The sufficient conditions (H'-bound, H'-tail, H'-pole-sep, H'-norm) are
   stated but not verified for the Suzuki sequence.

3. **Does not show Suzuki (H'-bound) or (H'-tail)**: this theorem only identifies the
   missing ingredients; it does not prove or disprove that Suzuki's framework satisfies them.

4. **Does not prove or disprove RH**: the non-uniqueness (E'-neg) is an obstacle to a
   class of convergence arguments; it does not say anything about the location of Riemann zeros.

5. **Meromorphic Hadamard uniqueness (Lemma E'.1)**: the proof sketch is correct but
   the parity/order constraint that forces `a_F = a_G` needs to be stated precisely
   for the specific case of W(z) (which is even in a suitable sense).
