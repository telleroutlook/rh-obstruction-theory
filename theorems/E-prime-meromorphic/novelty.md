# Novelty — Theorem E' (E-prime-meromorphic)

The E-neg non-uniqueness argument for the entire CCM target (Theorem E) is new.
E' extends this to the Suzuki meromorphic target. The meromorphic Hadamard uniqueness
lemma (E'.1) and the E'-neg construction are direct analogues of E-neg §3, adapted to
the pole/residue setting.

**Prior art gap:** The CCM entire target and the Suzuki meromorphic target are
explicitly different normalizations (CLAUDE.md and baseline/REFERENCE_BASELINE.md).
The extension from entire to meromorphic is non-trivial: Hurwitz is supplemented by a
contour/residue (argument-principle) step for the genuine simple pole, and the odd parity
of `W` forces an odd construction (Lemma E'.1 via `F/G` even). **Normal-family control
stays with Montel** on the holomorphy domain `Ω` (OB-11: (LB) gives local uniform
boundedness on a neighborhood of each compact, so Marty's spherical criterion is not
needed). The combined E/E' analysis gives the first systematic non-uniqueness +
sufficient-conditions treatment of both major spectral-approach normalizations.
