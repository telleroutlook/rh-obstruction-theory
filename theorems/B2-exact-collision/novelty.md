These results characterize when a truncated moment sequence has a unique vs. multiple
representing measures (flat extension / Tchakaloff). B2 is a concrete instance showing

and an off-line one. The novelty over pure moment theory is the explicit construction with
Ξ-like symmetry constraints (`ρ↦ρ̄`, `ρ↦1−ρ`) and exact integer multiplicities.
