## Closest prior results

### Curto–Fialkow / Bayer–Teichmann (truncated moment theory)

These results characterize when a truncated moment sequence has a unique vs.
multiple representing measures (flat extension / Tchakaloff).  B2 would be a
concrete instance showing that an *interior* moment vector (not flat) has a
representing measure off the critical line.  The novelty over pure moment theory
is the explicit construction with Ξ-like symmetry constraints and real counting.

### P21 (LEGACY PENDING)

As with B1, P21 may partially overlap.  The exact collision claim (zero tolerance)
is stronger than P21's stated scope; if P21 addressed only strict inequalities,
B2 would be new.
