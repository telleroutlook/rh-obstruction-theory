## Assessment

**Prior art:** Andersson (2408.15713) already proves prescribed-zero results
for Helson zeta functions.  The LITERATURE_MATRIX.md labels this OPEN-ish:

> standalone only if it adds a fixed-local-factor refinement to Andersson;
> else a section of Paper A.

**The new content** (if any) is the explicit statement that fixing finitely many
Euler factors to the standard value `χ(p) = 1` does not prevent off-line zeros,
combined with the observation that `R(s; P₀, χ)` is holomorphic and nonzero in
the critical strip.

**Verdict:**

| Scenario | Publication strategy |
|---|---|
| Andersson's theorem directly implies the P₀-standard conclusion with no new argument | Corollary; publish as a paragraph in Paper A |
| The fixed-local-factor step requires a new calculation (e.g. different function class, stronger normalization) | Short standalone note or §3 of Paper A |
| A new materially broader class (fixed local factors + functional equation) is handled | Standalone section of Paper A or separate paper |

**Current assessment:** likely a corollary / supporting section.  Standalone
publication requires Gate A verification of Andersson + a more refined claim
(e.g., zero at a specifically bounded height depending on P₀, or in the
Selberg class).
