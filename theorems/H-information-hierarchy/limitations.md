1. **Theorem H is a unification framework, not new mathematics.** Its content is
   exactly the union of B2 and G; all mathematical claims reduce to those theorems.

2. **Theorem H' is incomparability, not strict linear separation (corrected OB-27).** The
   earlier "`O_finite ⊊ O_theta`" total-chain claim was **false** and is withdrawn:
   `O_finite` and `O_theta` are **incomparable** observation maps (a same-imaginary-part pair
   is separated by `O_finite` but not `O_theta`; an S(T) ordinate move is separated by
   `O_theta` but not `O_finite`). Both are strict coarsenings of `O_oracle`. The coarsening
   half (part ii) inherits the exact B2/G collisions; the incomparability witnesses (part i)
   are explicit and script-verified but not yet independently checked.

3. **The O_vM layer is controlled by the G-hard conjecture** (not a theorem).

4. **Does not close any escape route.** The O_oracle layer is explicitly the escape —
   a method with access to all zero locations trivially avoids all obstructions.

5. **Does not prove or imply RH.**
